ABOUT THE ARTIST

About Me

Hi, I'm Geno Knowles, a intermediate artist exploring creativity through drawing, painting, and digital art. I have been interested in art from as long as I can remember, dating back to making Winx Club and Power Rangers artwork, to now creating digital illustrations and character designs. I enjoy experimenting with different techniques, and I am always looking for new ways to express myself through my art.

My artwork is inspired by a few great artists on Youtube, as I was a self-taught artist, and developed my skills and style through them. Some of the ones that immediately come to mind are RossDraws, Jazza, Sinix Design, Wlop, Marco Bucci and Angel Ganev.

Thank you for taking the time to look through my work.
